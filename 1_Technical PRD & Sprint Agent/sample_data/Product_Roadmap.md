# Product Roadmap

## Vision

Provide a scalable and secure customer support platform that improves customer experience and operational efficiency.

---

## Current Quarter Objectives

- Improve customer self-service.
- Enhance profile management.
- Improve ticket routing.
- Increase automation.
- Strengthen platform security.

---

## Planned Features

### Customer Profile Update

Priority: High

Allow customers to update:

- Name
- Phone Number
- Notification Preferences

---

### AI Ticket Classification

Priority: High

Automatically classify incoming support tickets.

---

### Notification Improvements

Priority: Medium

Support Email Notifications

Support Chat Notifications

---

### Reporting Dashboard

Priority: Medium

Provide operational dashboards for support managers.

---

## Out of Scope

- Mobile Application
- Third-party CRM Integration

---

## Success Metrics

- Reduced support tickets
- Faster ticket resolution
- Increased customer satisfaction
